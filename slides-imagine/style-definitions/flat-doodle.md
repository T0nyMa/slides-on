# Flat Doodle — AI Image Style Definition

Playful flat vector doodle style with rounded corners, simple icons, and toy model aesthetic. Bright solid colors, no gradients, cheerful and approachable.

## Style Lock (跨页复用)

```text
Playful flat vector doodle illustration with solid bright colors and rounded shapes.
Rounded rectangle shapes (corner radius 12–16px) in solid fill — no gradients, no shadows, no 3D.
Simple iconography: stick figures, basic shapes, cartoon objects — drawn as if by a friendly whiteboard marker.
Line work: uniform 1.5pt outline strokes in dark gray (#333333) around all shapes.
Colors: bright saturated flats — sky blue (#4FC3F7), warm yellow (#FFD54F), coral pink (#FF8A80), mint green (#81C784), lavender (#B39DDB).
White (#FFFFFF) as spacing and negative space — no colored backgrounds, white canvas.
Toy model aesthetic — everything looks slightly chunky, friendly, and approachable.
Scattered decorative elements: stars, dots, squiggles, small hearts as visual filler.
```

## Visual Elements

- Rounded rectangles in solid bright colors
- 1.5pt uniform dark gray outlines on all shapes
- Simple stick figures and cartoon objects
- Decorative scatter elements (stars, dots, squiggles)
- White background only — no colored canvas
- Chunky, approachable proportions
- NO gradients, shadows, or 3D effects

## Typography

- Rounded, friendly sans-serif — like a whiteboard marker
- Title: bold, slightly oversized
- Labels: regular weight, readable at small sizes
- NO serif, NO formal, NO thin fonts

## Composition

- White canvas with generous margins
- Elements arranged in a loose grid with playful offsets
- Decorative scatter fills dead space naturally
- Centered or slightly off-center main subject
- Light, airy, not crowded

## Negative Constraints

- NO gradients, NO drop shadows, NO 3D
- NO dark backgrounds — always white canvas
- NO realistic textures, NO photorealistic elements
- NO thin/formal fonts
- NO more than 5 distinct colors per illustration
