# Retro — AI Image Style Definition

Vintage poster aesthetics with muted color palettes, retro typography, and grain texture. Mid-century modern meets 70s editorial. Warm, nostalgic, textured.

## Visual Elements

- Grain texture overlay (15–25% noise)
- Geometric retro shapes (circles, diamonds, starbursts, ellipses)
- Halftone dot shading
- Thick warm brown borders with rounded corners
- Sunburst rays, atomic dots, boomerang curves
- Aged cream paper stock
- NO digital smoothness or perfect gradients

## Typography

- Mid-century modern typography
- Bold condensed headlines
- Serif body text with character
- NO digital sans-serif, NO Helvetica, NO thin modern fonts
- Letter spacing slightly loose for headlines

## Composition

- Full-bleed retro poster layout
- Central geometric focal element
- Sunburst or ray backgrounds behind main subject
- Thick border frame with rounded corners
- Balanced but dynamic — retro is confident, not timid

## Negative Constraints

- NO pure white — always aged cream or warm tone
- NO neon or digital-bright colors
- NO smooth digital gradients
- NO thin modern fonts
- NO photorealistic 3D renders
- NO pastel — retro is muted but saturated
