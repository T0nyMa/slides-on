# Minimal — AI Image Style Definition

Ultra-clean minimalism. Pure white backgrounds, thin black monoline illustrations, maximum whitespace, and absolute restraint. Japanese and Scandinavian design philosophy — less is more.

## Style Lock (跨页复用)

```text
Ultra-minimal black-on-white illustration on pure white background (#FFFFFF).
Single-weight monoline drawing: thin black (#1A1A1A) lines at consistent 0.5–1pt visual weight throughout.
No fills, no shading, no gradients — pure line art. Generous negative space: 60–75% of canvas is untouched white.
One single accent color per image, used sparingly: muted coral #D4958A, warm sand #C4B5A0, soft sage #A3B5A0, or pale sky #B0C5D5.
All shapes rendered as clean continuous-line outlines — circles, rounded rectangles, simple geometric forms.
Dot-grid or subtle line-grid background pattern optional (2–3% opacity, #E0E0E0) for spatial reference.
Subject drawn with economy of line: minimum strokes to convey form, no decorative flourishes, no hatching.
Fixed shell: pure white background, thin monoline style, dot-grid optional, accent color consistent per page.
No heavy fills, no thick lines, no shadows, no gradients, no textures, no busy compositions, no decorative elements.
```

## Visual Elements

- Thin monoline black outlines — consistent single weight, no variation in stroke thickness
- Continuous-line drawing style: forms rendered with minimal, flowing strokes
- Simple geometric shapes: circles, rounded rectangles, straight lines, gentle arcs
- One accent color per image used as a small fill area or highlight dot
- Optional subtle dot-grid background (2–3% opacity) to anchor the composition
- Abstract representations of concepts: simplified icons, minimal glyphs, geometric metaphors
- Plant or organic forms reduced to essential line shapes (single-line branch, circle fruit)
- Architectural or spatial elements: single-line horizon, minimal room outline, floating plane
- Hand-drawn quality in line edges — slightly imperfect, not cold mechanical vector
- NO filled shapes (except the one accent spot), NO gradients, NO textures

## Typography

- ALL text as clean geometric sans-serif lettering — Helvetica/Arial/Inter aesthetic
- Main titles: thin weight (300), upper area, left-aligned or centered, black
- Keywords: medium weight (500), same font family, with thin underline in the accent color
- Labels: small, light weight, positioned near relevant diagram elements
- Text must appear hand-rendered with slight imperfection — not computer-perfect
- NO serif fonts — maintain geometric sans-serif consistency
- NO decorative typography, NO mixed font families
- Title: 3–8 words, short and punchy
- Labels: 2–5 per page, 1–4 words each

## Composition

- Maximum whitespace: 60–75% of canvas is untouched white
- Central subject occupies only 20–30% of canvas — small and deliberate
- Strong alignment to grid or rule-of-thirds anchor points
- Asymmetric balance: subject weighted to one side or corner
- Fixed shell: pure white background, monoline style, accent color choice
- Vary only the central minimal subject and accent placement across pages
- Text and illustration areas clearly separated — no overlap
- Bottom area often left completely empty for visual breathing room

## Negative Constraints

- NO fills on shapes (except the single accent area per image)
- NO gradients, NO shadows, NO textures, NO patterns (except dot-grid)
- NO thick lines — everything is thin monoline (0.5–1pt visual weight)
- NO multiple line weights or varied stroke styles
- NO decorative flourishes, NO ornamental elements
- NO busy compositions, NO multiple focal points
- NO photorealistic elements, NO 3D rendering
- NO dark backgrounds — always pure white (#FFFFFF)
- NO saturated colors — accent colors are muted and restrained
- NO heavy typography or long text blocks
- NO clip art aesthetic — the minimalism must feel premium and intentional
