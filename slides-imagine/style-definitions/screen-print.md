# Screen Print — AI Image Style Definition

Bold poster aesthetic with limited color layers and halftone dot textures. Screen printing feel — registration marks, slight color misalignment, spot color overlays. High contrast, graphic, impactful.

## Style Lock (跨页复用)

```text
Bold screen-printed poster aesthetic with limited color layers (3–5 colors max).
Halftone dot textures visible on all color blocks — cyan, magenta, yellow dots at 45° angles.
Slight registration misalignment between color layers (1–2px offset) — authentic screen print feel.
Registration marks (crosshair with circle) in corners.
Spot color overlays: colors overlap with multiply blend, creating secondary tones.
High contrast compositions — large areas of flat color with bold graphic shapes.
Poster-style typography: bold, condensed, impactful.
Paper texture: off-white (#F5F0E8) with slight tooth.
Borders: uneven ink coverage at edges, slight bleed.
```

## Visual Elements

- 3-5 color layers max
- Halftone dot textures on all filled areas
- Registration marks in corners
- Slight color misalignment for authenticity
- Spot color overlays with multiply blend
- Bold graphic shapes (circles, bars, stripes)
- Uneven ink edges
- Off-white paper texture with tooth

## Typography

- Bold condensed poster fonts
- Large display type for headlines
- All-caps short labels
- Slight ink bleed at letter edges
- NO thin/light fonts, NO italic, NO decorative scripts

## Composition

- High contrast, graphic, poster-like
- Large flat color areas with bold shapes
- Registration marks define the print boundary
- Centered or asymmetric — always dramatic
- 3-color minimal or 5-color rich — no more

## Negative Constraints

- NO more than 5 distinct colors
- NO gradients, NO smooth blends, NO digital effects
- NO photorealistic, NO 3D, NO soft shadows
- NO thin lines (under 2pt equivalent)
- NO pastel or muted — this is BOLD
