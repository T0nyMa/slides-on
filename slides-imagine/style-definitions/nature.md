# Nature — AI Image Style Definition

Earth-toned botanical illustration style with organic shapes and leaf/flower motifs. Warm natural palette — greens, browns, ochres. Watercolor wash textures, delicate botanical line work.

## Style Lock (跨页复用)

```text
Botanical illustration style on warm cream paper (#F5F0E0).
Earth-tone palette: forest green (#2E7D32), olive (#689F38), warm brown (#795548), ochre (#D4A24E), sage (#A5D6A7).
Delicate botanical line work — thin 0.5–1pt lines with natural organic curves.
Watercolor wash textures on leaves and decorative elements — transparent, layered, organic bleed.
Botanical motifs: leaves, branches, ferns, flowers as decorative borders and section dividers.
Organic shapes — no perfect circles, natural asymmetry, living forms.
Paper texture: warm, slight grain, natural fiber feel.
Vignette edges — slightly darker at corners, fading inward.
```

## Visual Elements

- Delicate botanical line work (0.5–1pt)
- Watercolor wash textures on leaves and decorative elements
- Leaf, branch, fern, and flower motifs as borders/dividers
- Organic asymmetric shapes — no mechanical symmetry
- Vignette edges on warm cream paper
- Natural fiber paper texture

## Typography

- Hand-lettered botanical style — organic, warm
- Serif for formal botanical labels
- NO sans-serif, NO digital/mechanical fonts
- Warm ink colors — brown or dark green, never pure black

## Composition

- Warm cream canvas with vignette edges
- Botanical border elements frame the content naturally
- Organic flow — no rigid grid
- Central composition with natural balance
- Breathing room — nature needs space

## Negative Constraints

- NO pure white backgrounds — always warm cream
- NO mechanical/geometric shapes — everything is organic
- NO neon or synthetic colors — earth tones only
- NO pure black (#000000) — use dark brown instead
- NO digital effects — this is botanical, not digital
