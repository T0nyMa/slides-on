# Bold Editorial — AI Image Style Definition

Bold magazine editorial style with high-contrast compositions, striking typography, and confident color blocking. Evokes Vogue, Monocle, or Kinfolk editorial spreads — fashion-forward, opinionated, visually loud.

## Style Lock (跨页复用)

```text
Bold editorial magazine aesthetic. High-contrast color blocking: large flat areas of deep black (#0D0D0D), crisp white (#FFFFFF), or bold accent blocks.
Confident accent palette: editorial red #D32F2F, mustard yellow #F5A623, electric cobalt #2D5FCF, hot coral #FF6B6B, acid green #C0DE00.
Split-page compositions: 40/60 or 50/50 color blocks with sharp vertical or diagonal divisions.
Oversized geometric shapes as graphic anchors: giant circles, bold diagonals, massive typography as visual elements.
High-impact duotone effect on any photographic elements: black + one accent color, stark and graphic.
Bold solid-color panels behind key content — no gradients, no wishy-washy transitions.
Large-scale typography used as graphic design elements: single words or numbers spanning 30–40% of canvas.
Thick rules, oversized bullets, and dramatic negative space (but intentional, not minimal — negative space is a design weapon).
No subtlety, no pastels, no soft transitions — every visual choice is deliberate, loud, and confident.
```

## Visual Elements

- Bold solid-color blocks occupying large portions of the canvas (30–60% area)
- Sharp geometric divisions: vertical splits, diagonal cuts, asymmetric color fields
- Oversized geometric shapes: massive circles, triangles, and rectangles as graphic anchors
- Duotone photographic elements: black-and-white imagery overlaid with one bold accent color
- Large-scale typography as visual elements: single words or numbers filling 30–40% of the page
- Thick black rules and borders (4–8pt visual weight) for dramatic separation
- Abstract graphic patterns: bold stripes, oversized dots, Memphis-inspired geometric arrangements
- Negative space used as an active design tool — vast empty areas that amplify the bold elements
- Collage aesthetic elements: overlapping shapes, offset layers, intentional misalignment
- High-contrast silhouettes and simplified graphic forms — no fine detail, everything reads at thumbnail size

## Typography

- ALL text as bold, confident typography — editorial serif or geometric sans-serif
- Main titles: extra-bold or black weight (800–900), full-width or near-full-width, dramatic scale
- Keywords and pull quotes: oversized, set in contrasting color blocks, acting as graphic elements
- Body text: clean sans-serif, well-spaced, generous leading — but minimal in quantity
- Mixed type families allowed: serif headlines + sans-serif body, or vice versa
- Letter-spacing: titles often tracked-out (positive letter-spacing) for editorial feel
- NO timid typography — if text is present, it's loud and intentional
- Title: 2–6 words, maximum impact, often single bold statement
- Pull quotes: 1 per page max, large display, in contrasting color block

## Composition

- Split-page compositions: 40/60 or 50/50 color field divisions
- Asymmetric balance: heavy element on one side, vast empty space on the other
- Oversized elements that bleed off the page edge — confident cropping
- Grid-breaking layouts: intentional misalignment, offset layers, overlapping forms
- Fixed shell: editorial attitude, alternating color-block structure, bold rule style
- Vary accent color, split position, and focal element across pages
- Photography (if present) always treated as duotone — never full-color
- Every page should feel like a magazine spread — not a slide, not a document

## Negative Constraints

- NO pastel colors — editorial is bold, saturated, and confident
- NO gradients — all colors are flat solid blocks
- NO soft transitions, NO fade effects, NO wishy-washy blends
- NO delicate linework or fine detail — everything must read at distance
- NO timid typography — every text element is large, bold, and intentional
- NO cluttered compositions — bold style relies on negative space for impact
- NO default PowerPoint/Keynote template look — this is magazine editorial
- NO multiple small elements scattered on the page — fewer elements, bigger scale
- NO photorealistic imagery in full color — always duotone treatment
- NO conservative, safe design choices — editorial is opinionated and takes risks
- NO inconsistent color usage — one dominant accent per page, maximum two
