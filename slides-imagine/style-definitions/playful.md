# Playful — AI Image Style Definition

Bright vibrant colors, cartoon expressions, fun shapes, and energetic composition. Bubble letters, star bursts, confetti elements. Designed for joy and engagement.

```text

Cartoon expressions: smiling faces, winking eyes, excited sparkle eyes on characters.
Decorative elements: star bursts, confetti dots, action lines, speech bubbles, thought clouds.
Bouncy composition — elements feel like they're floating/hopping on the page.
Gradient fills allowed but kept simple (2-color max per gradient).
Background: either white or one bright solid color as a statement.
No straight lines — everything has slight curve, bounce, or tilt.
```

## Visual Elements

- 2–3pt bold black outlines on all major shapes
- Cartoon expressions and characters
- Star bursts, confetti, action lines
- Speech bubbles and thought clouds
- Simple 2-color gradients
- Bouncy, tilted, energetic composition
- NO sharp corners — everything rounded

## Typography

- Bubble letters or rounded comic fonts for titles
- Playful, bouncy baselines — letters don't sit perfectly straight
- Bold weight for emphasis
- NO serif, NO formal, NO thin fonts
- Speech bubble text in comic lettering style

## Composition

- High energy — elements burst from center or sweep diagonally
- Generous spacing between fun elements
- One large focal point + many small decorative scatter elements
- Bright backgrounds or white with color pops
- Always energetic, never static

## Negative Constraints

- NO straight/static layouts — must feel alive
- NO muted/pastel — colors are BRIGHT
- NO formal typography
- NO photorealistic elements
- NO dark/moody atmosphere
- NO more than 6 distinct colors + black outlines
