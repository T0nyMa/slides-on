# Funnel

**Best for**: Conversion funnels, filtering processes, and narrowing-down sequences
**Structure**: Horizontal bands that narrow from top to bottom, forming a funnel shape. Each band represents a stage, with the width proportional to the quantity at that stage. Arrows or connectors between stages
**Elements**: Tapered bands (widest at top, narrowest at bottom), stage labels, quantity/percentage indicators, conversion rate labels between stages, drop-off annotations

## Content Requirements
- 3-7 sequential stages where quantity decreases at each step
- Quantity or percentage at each stage (required for the visual taper)
- Optional: conversion rates or drop-off percentages between stages
- Stages must follow a filtering/narrowing logic

## When to Use
- Sales or marketing conversion funnels
- Data filtering or processing pipelines
- Candidate selection or screening processes
- Any process where the pool shrinks at each successive stage

## When NOT to Use
- Processes where quantity does not decrease — use linear-progression
- When stage quantities are unknown or not meaningful
- Reverse processes (expanding rather than narrowing) — consider an inverted layout
