# Story Mountain

**Best for**: Story arcs, narrative journeys, and dramatic structures
**Structure**: A rising and falling curve (mountain shape) that traces a narrative arc from exposition through rising action to climax and falling action to resolution. Nodes mark key plot points along the curve
**Elements**: Mountain curve line, plot point nodes (exposition, rising action, climax, falling action, resolution), elevation indicators, labels at each stage

## Content Requirements
- A narrative with a clear dramatic arc (not all stories fit this structure)
- 5-7 key plot points mapping to the classic story structure
- A definite climax or turning point
- Content should be story-driven rather than data-driven

## When to Use
- User journey narratives with emotional highs and lows
- Project retrospectives or "how we got here" stories
- Brand storytelling and origin narratives
- Explaining complex events through narrative structure

## When NOT to Use
- Linear processes without dramatic tension — use linear-progression
- Data-driven or metric-focused content — use dashboard or charts
- When the narrative is flat (no rising/falling tension)
- Cyclical narratives — use circular-flow
