# Structural Breakdown

**Best for**: Component breakdowns, anatomy diagrams, and exploded views
**Structure**: An "exploded" or disassembled view where the whole object is shown separated into its constituent parts, with callout lines connecting each part to its label. Parts are positioned to suggest how they fit together
**Elements**: Exploded/disassembled object, component parts, callout lines connecting parts to labels, part labels and descriptions, optional reassembly arrows

## Content Requirements
- A complex system or object to decompose (3-10 components)
- Each component: a name, a descriptive label, and a visual representation
- Clear spatial relationship showing how parts fit together
- Optional: the assembled whole shown alongside or above

## When to Use
- System architecture decomposition
- Product anatomy or "what's inside" diagrams
- Breaking down a complex concept into constituent ideas
- Methodology or framework component explanation

## When NOT to Use
- Sequential workflows — use linear-progression
- Abstract relationships without spatial/structural meaning
- When components are purely conceptual with no "fit together" relationship
