# Binary Comparison

**Best for**: Side-by-side comparison of two options, approaches, or states
**Structure**: Two columns (left/right) with a clear divider, each side mirroring the other's structure for easy comparison. Optionally includes a center "vs" label
**Elements**: Two labeled columns, paired comparison rows, check/cross indicators, highlights for differences

## Content Requirements
- Exactly two things to compare (A vs B, before vs after, option 1 vs option 2)
- Comparable attributes — same dimensions for both sides
- Clear differentiating features to highlight

## When to Use
- Comparing two competing approaches, technologies, or strategies
- Before/after transformation scenarios
- Pros vs cons when grouped into two clear positions

## When NOT to Use
- Comparing three or more items — use comparison-matrix instead
- When the two sides are not meaningfully comparable on the same dimensions
- When you need to show gradual progression rather than binary opposition
