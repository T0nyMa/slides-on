# Morandi Journal

**Look**: Quiet, contemplative style inspired by Giorgio Morandi's still-life paintings, with muted tones and a journal/notebook aesthetic
**Palette**: Dusty muted tones (sage green, dusty rose, pale ochre, slate gray, warm cream), low saturation
**Mood**: Quiet, contemplative, refined, understated, meditative

## Characteristics
- Texture: Soft matte surfaces, subtle paper grain, gentle brushstrokes, powder-like finish
- Lines: Soft edges, gentle contours, subtle transitions, no harsh boundaries
- Typography: Elegant thin serif, generous leading, quiet hierarchy, handwritten accents

## When to Use
- Reflective or philosophical content
- Creative writing or poetry presentations
- Brand stories emphasizing subtlety and refinement
- When the content benefits from a calm, unhurried visual pace
