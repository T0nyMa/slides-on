# Subway Map

**Look**: Transit/subway map diagram style with colored route lines, station dots, and geometric 45/90-degree angles
**Palette**: White/light background, 6-8 distinct route colors (like transit lines), black station dots, white station labels
**Mood**: Systematic, navigational, information-dense, urban, organized

## Characteristics
- Texture: Flat and clean, no gradients, pure information-design clarity
- Lines: Fixed-weight colored routes, strictly 45/90-degree angles, parallel lines with even gaps, interchange station connectors
- Typography: Condensed sans-serif station labels, consistent label placement, route-number badges

## When to Use
- Process or workflow maps with multiple parallel paths
- System overviews with interconnected routes
- Information architecture or navigation design
- When showing how multiple paths converge and diverge
