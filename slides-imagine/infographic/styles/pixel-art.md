# Pixel Art

**Look**: 8-bit/16-bit retro pixel art with blocky pixel grids, limited color palettes, and gaming nostalgia
**Palette**: Retro gaming palettes (NES/SNES-inspired), limited indexed colors, dithering patterns
**Mood**: Nostalgic, retro, playful, digital-native, indie-game charm

## Characteristics
- Texture: Visible pixel grid, dithering patterns, blocky aliased edges, sprite-sheet aesthetic
- Lines: Staircase pixel edges, 1px outlines, tile-based grid alignment, sprite boundaries
- Typography: Blocky pixel fonts, fixed-width character cells, arcade high-score styling

## When to Use
- Retro gaming or digital nostalgia topics
- Indie tech or startup culture presentations
- Explaining digital concepts in a playful way
- When the audience is tech-savvy and appreciates retro aesthetics
