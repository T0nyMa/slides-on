# Claymation

**Look**: Clay or plasticine stop-motion animation aesthetic with soft, rounded, tactile forms
**Palette**: Bright, saturated modeling clay colors (primary red, blue, yellow, green), slightly matte finish
**Mood**: Playful, tactile, whimsical, childlike wonder

## Characteristics
- Texture: Soft clay surface with subtle fingerprints, slight irregularities, matte finish
- Lines: Rounded organic shapes, no sharp edges, soft shadows, plump proportions
- Typography: Rounded bubble letters, 3D extruded clay letterforms, playful proportions

## When to Use
- Creative or children-focused content
- Explaining complex topics in an approachable way
- Brand storytelling with a playful personality
- When you want to soften a technical or serious topic
