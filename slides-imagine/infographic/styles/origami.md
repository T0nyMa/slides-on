# Origami

**Look**: Origami paper craft aesthetic with folded paper surfaces, crisp crease lines, and angular geometric forms
**Palette**: Japanese washi paper colors (vermillion, indigo, gold, muted green, cream), subtle color variation on faces
**Mood**: Precise, artistic, transformative, disciplined, elegant

## Characteristics
- Texture: Paper fiber subtle texture, slight translucency at folds, matte paper surface, subtle shadow at creases
- Lines: Sharp crease lines, precise geometric edges, triangular facet boundaries, fold pattern diagrams
- Typography: Clean sans-serif, angular characters echoing folded forms, subtle shadow depth

## When to Use
- Transformation or change management topics
- Complex-to-simple distillation presentations
- Design thinking or creative process explanations
- Japanese culture or methodology topics
