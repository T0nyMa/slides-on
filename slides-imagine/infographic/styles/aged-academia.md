# Aged Academia

**Look**: Vintage academic textbooks with yellowed paper, engraved illustrations, and classical typography
**Palette**: Warm parchment tones (cream, sepia, ochre), faded ink blacks, muted burgundy accents
**Mood**: Scholarly, timeless, authoritative, contemplative

## Characteristics
- Texture: Aged paper grain, yellowed pages, slight foxing spots, worn edges
- Lines: Fine engraved linework, cross-hatching, detailed ornamental borders
- Typography: Classical serif, old-style figures, drop caps, letterpress impression

## When to Use
- Historical or philosophical topics
- Classical literature or humanities presentations
- When authority and tradition need visual emphasis
- Academic lectures with historical grounding
