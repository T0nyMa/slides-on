# Technical Schematic

**Look**: Technical schematic and blueprint style with white-on-blue linework, precise measurements, and engineering annotations
**Palette**: Blueprint blue background (#1a3a5c or similar), white lines, occasional yellow/red annotation accents
**Mood**: Technical, precise, engineering-focused, authoritative, systematic

## Characteristics
- Texture: Blueprint paper grain, diazo reproduction artifacts, grid overlay, title block and border
- Lines: Precise drafting lines, dimension arrows, centerlines, hidden-line dashes, measurement callouts
- Typography: Technical drafting lettering (all-caps sans-serif), title block formatting, dimension annotations, specification tables

## When to Use
- Engineering or architecture presentations
- System design and technical specification documents
- Patent or invention disclosures
- When precision and technical credibility are paramount
