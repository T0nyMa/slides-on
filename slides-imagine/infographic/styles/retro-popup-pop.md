# Retro Popup Pop

**Look**: Retro pop-up book style with layered paper depth, die-cut windows, and dimensional page elements
**Palette**: Vintage children's book colors (warm reds, golden yellows, sky blues), layered paper shadows, cream page bases
**Mood**: Magical, dimensional, surprising, nostalgic, storybook wonder

## Characteristics
- Texture: Paper layers with visible thickness, die-cut edges, fold lines, pop-up tab shadows, page spread creases
- Lines: Fold score lines, tab-and-slot mechanisms, layered silhouette edges, page-turn curves
- Typography: Storybook serif, dimensional drop shadows, stand-up lettering, page-number styling

## When to Use
- Storytelling or narrative-driven presentations
- Product reveals or "unfolding" announcements
- Children's content or family-oriented brands
- When you want to create a sense of discovery and delight
