# Knolling

**Look**: Top-down organized flat lay photography where items are arranged at right angles with meticulous spacing
**Palette**: Neutral background surfaces (white, gray, wood), items in their natural colors, consistent lighting
**Mood**: Organized, meticulous, satisfying, analytical, transparent

## Characteristics
- Texture: Photographic realism, surface textures (wood grain, concrete, fabric), subtle shadows
- Lines: Strict 90-degree alignment, even spacing, grid-based arrangement, orthogonal perspective
- Typography: Clean label cards, museum-style object tags, minimal sans-serif captions

## When to Use
- Product teardowns or component showcases
- "What's in the box" or kit contents presentations
- Tool or equipment inventories
- When transparency and organization are the message
