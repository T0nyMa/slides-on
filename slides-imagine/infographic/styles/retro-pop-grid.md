# Retro Pop Grid

**Look**: Retro pop art with strong grid-based layouts, bold color blocking, and vintage print advertising aesthetic
**Palette**: Vintage pop colors (tomato red, mustard yellow, teal, cream), offset-print CMYK feel, halftone overlays
**Mood**: Bold, graphic, vintage-cool, advertising-chic, pop-culture savvy

## Characteristics
- Texture: Halftone dot patterns, offset-print misregistration, newsprint grain, screen-print ink texture
- Lines: Strong grid lines, ruled borders, geometric window panels, registration marks
- Typography: Vintage advertising fonts, condensed sans-serif, bold slab serif headers, WANTED-poster hierarchy

## When to Use
- Marketing or advertising campaign presentations
- Pop culture or media analysis topics
- Brand identity or design system showcases
- When bold visual impact with vintage flair is desired
