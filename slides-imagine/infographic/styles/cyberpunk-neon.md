# Cyberpunk Neon

**Look**: Neon lights glowing against dark cyberpunk cityscapes, high-tech low-life aesthetic
**Palette**: Deep black/purple backgrounds, neon cyan, magenta, yellow, and green glowing elements
**Mood**: Futuristic, edgy, high-energy, dystopian-cool

## Characteristics
- Texture: Glow and bloom effects, rain-slicked surfaces, holographic sheen, grid patterns
- Lines: Glowing neon tubes, circuit traces, sharp angular geometry, wireframe meshes
- Typography: Neon tube lettering, monospace tech fonts, glitch effects, holographic overlays

## When to Use
- Futuristic or sci-fi themed content
- Cybersecurity or hacking-related presentations
- Gaming or esports topics
- When you want a dramatic, attention-grabbing visual style
