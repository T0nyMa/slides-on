# Bold Graphic

**Look**: Bold flat graphics with strong colors, thick outlines, and high-contrast compositions
**Palette**: Vibrant primaries and secondaries, high saturation, strong contrast, limited palette (3-5 colors)
**Mood**: Confident, modern, energetic, impactful

## Characteristics
- Texture: Flat, no gradients, solid color fills
- Lines: Thick uniform outlines, geometric precision, clean edges
- Typography: Bold sans-serif, heavy weights, all-caps headers, large display type

## When to Use
- Product launches and marketing presentations
- Data storytelling with strong visual impact
- When the message needs to feel decisive and clear
- Modern tech or startup pitch decks
