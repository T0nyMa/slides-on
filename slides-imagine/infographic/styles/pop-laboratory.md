# Pop Laboratory

**Look**: Pop art aesthetic meets science laboratory, with bold halftone patterns, lab equipment, and vibrant comic-style energy
**Palette**: Pop art primaries (bright yellow, cyan, magenta), lab white, chrome/silver accents, comic-book black
**Mood**: Experimental, energetic, inventive, irreverent, "mad scientist" charm

## Characteristics
- Texture: Ben-Day dots / halftone patterns, glossy lab surfaces, bubble textures, splatter effects
- Lines: Bold comic-book outlines, Erlenmeyer curves, grid paper backgrounds, dynamic action lines
- Typography: Comic-book bold, pop art speech bubbles, stencil lab labels, exaggerated punctuation

## When to Use
- R&D or innovation presentations
- Experimental results or "lab findings"
- Science communication with a fun twist
- Hackathon or maker project presentations
