# Chalkboard

**Look**: Dark green or black chalkboard surface with chalk-drawn illustrations and handwritten text
**Palette**: Dark green or black background, white and pastel chalk colors (dusty pink, pale yellow, light blue)
**Mood**: Educational, approachable, nostalgic, hands-on

## Characteristics
- Texture: Chalk dust, smudged areas, uneven coverage, erased ghost marks
- Lines: Variable width chalk strokes, slightly wobbly, textured edges, dot-dash patterns
- Typography: Handwritten chalk lettering, casual cursive, uneven baseline, chalk dust halo

## When to Use
- Educational and teaching content
- Workshop or classroom presentations
- Brainstorming or ideation sessions
- When a personal, handcrafted feel is desired
