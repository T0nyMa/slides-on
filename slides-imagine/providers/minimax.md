# MiniMax (海螺AI)

**API**: `api.minimax.chat` (HTTP, requires MiniMax API key)
**Models**: `image-01` (text-to-image)
**Strengths**: Excellent photorealism, fast generation speed, good at detailed scene composition
**Limitations**: Fewer artistic/stylized options, some content moderation restricts certain subjects, less flexible aspect ratio support than Midjourney

## Capabilities
- Text-to-image: yes
- Reference image: no
- Aspect ratios: 1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 3:4
- Resolution: up to 1024x1024

## Best For
- Photorealistic illustrations for tech decks (devices, environments, people)
- Product concept mockups and scene visualizations
- When Midjourney-level photorealism is needed but at lower cost and faster turnaround

## Prompt Tips
- Use English prompts for best photorealism results
- Be specific about lighting, camera angle, and material textures
- Add negative prompts for unwanted artifacts (e.g., "watermark, distorted faces, extra limbs")

## Cost/Performance
- Speed: fast (typically 2-6s per generation)
- Cost: low (pay-per-call, competitive with DashScope)
