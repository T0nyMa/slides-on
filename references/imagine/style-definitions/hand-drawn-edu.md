# Hand-Drawn Education — AI Image Style Definition

Friendly educational illustration style. Paper notebook background, marker pen colors, hand-drawn borders, simulating whiteboard/notebook teaching aesthetics. Warm and inviting, lowers the psychological barrier for learning.

## Style Lock (跨页复用)

```text
Refined Chinese handdrawn educational illustration on off-white paper (near #FBFAF5) with subtle grain.
No full-page border. Near-black ink (#111111) linework — hand-drawn, slightly irregular, friendly pen-on-paper feel.
Optional faint notebook ruled lines in background. Simple rounded-rect borders around key content blocks.
Marker pen accents: warm orange #F48B5C, sky blue #4E8ED6, sage green #2E9D70, soft purple #7B5DC4.
Pale yellow #FDF0B2 highlighter marks behind keywords. Colored marker underlines below key terms.
Generous margins (35–45% whitespace). Central illustration 50–60% page width.
Simple hand-drawn icons: lightbulb, pencil, book, gear — small and sparse.
Curved hand-drawn arrow connectors with small arrowheads. Dotted-line underlines. Checkmarks with marker feel.
At most one tiny stick-figure character (teacher/student), far corner.
No photorealistic, no 3D, no gradients, no shadows, no neon, no thick permanent-marker strokes, no bullet lists, no corporate template feel, no yellow or beige paper.
```

## Visual Elements

- Hand-drawn slightly irregular linework — stable but not mechanical
- Notebook ruled-line decoration (faint horizontal lines in background, optional)
- Simple hand-drawn borders (rounded corners, single stroke) around key content blocks
- Stick-figure characters (teacher at whiteboard, student at desk) — max 1 per page
- Simple icons: lightbulb (idea), pencil (learning), book (knowledge), gear (mechanism)
- Arrow connectors: curved hand-drawn lines with small arrowheads
- Checkmarks and cross marks drawn with marker pen feel
- Dotted-line underlines below key terms
- Decorative elements sparse: small stars, simple squiggles at corners only
- Paper texture: subtle grain on off-white background

## Typography

- ALL text hand-drawn / handwritten style, natural pen-on-paper feel
- Main titles: medium-large, friendly hand-lettering, warm and inviting
- Keywords: enlarged + colored marker underline (not highlighter block)
- Labels: small, neat hand-drawn inside rounded marker-label boxes
- NO printed fonts, NO computer-generated text
- NO calligraphic brush script — keep it simple pen writing
- Title: 5–12 Chinese characters
- Labels: 2–5 per page, 3–8 characters each

## Composition

- Generous margins, calm negative space (35–45% whitespace)
- Central illustration occupies 50–60% page width
- Title top-aligned with simple hand-drawn underline
- Optional notebook ruled lines as subtle background pattern
- Page number small, upper-left, handwritten
- Fixed visual shell: paper tone, title style, line weight, palette — vary only central diagram

## Negative Constraints

- NO full-page border
- NO photorealistic, NO 3D render, NO computer-generated look
- NO gradients, NO shadows, NO neon colors
- NO thick permanent-marker strokes (use fine pen + light marker fills)
- NO many characters (max 1 tiny figure)
- NO bullet lists, NO dense paragraphs
- NO corporate template feel, NO clip art
- NO yellow or beige paper — keep near-white warm tone
- NO fake English, NO filler text, NO watermarks
