# Bridge

**Best for**: Connecting two concepts, domains, or entities via a bridging mechanism
**Structure**: Two land masses or platforms on left and right, connected by a bridge structure in the center. The bridge itself can carry labels, arrows, or data flows
**Elements**: Left and right anchors, bridge structure, connection labels, flow indicators (arrows), optional gap/chasm visual

## Content Requirements
- Two distinct domains or concepts that need to be connected
- A bridging mechanism or shared connection to describe
- Optional: what flows across the bridge (data, value, communication)

## When to Use
- Explaining how two systems or teams integrate or communicate
- Describing partnerships, APIs, or integration layers
- Showing how a solution bridges a gap between problem and resolution

## When NOT to Use
- When there is only one central entity with outbound connections — use hub-spoke
- When the connection is purely sequential — use linear-progression
- When the "bridge" is metaphorical with no clear left/right anchors
