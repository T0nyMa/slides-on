# Jigsaw

**Best for**: Showing how parts fit together, team structures, and interdependent components
**Structure**: Interlocking puzzle pieces arranged to form a complete whole. Each piece represents a component, and the interlocking tabs show how they connect. Pieces can be separated (exploded) or assembled
**Elements**: Puzzle piece shapes with interlocking tabs, piece labels, connecting arrows (if exploded), completed outline (ghost shape showing the whole)

## Content Requirements
- 3-8 components that together form a complete system or solution
- Each piece should have a clear name and role
- The interlocking metaphor must hold: pieces are interdependent and form a coherent whole when assembled
- Optional: show both exploded and assembled views

## When to Use
- Team structure where roles are complementary and interdependent
- System components that integrate to form a complete solution
- Strategy frameworks where each pillar is necessary but insufficient alone
- Partnership or collaboration models

## When NOT to Use
- Independent or loosely-coupled components — use bento-grid
- Sequential processes where pieces don't interlock — use linear-progression
- When pieces are purely additive (sum of parts) rather than interlocking
