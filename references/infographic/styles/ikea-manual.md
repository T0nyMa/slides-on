# IKEA Manual

**Look**: Clean, wordless line-art instructional diagrams in the style of flat-pack furniture assembly guides
**Palette**: Crisp white background, solid black line-art, occasional yellow or orange safety/alert accents
**Mood**: Universal, clear, instructional, no-nonsense

## Characteristics
- Texture: Completely flat, no shading or gradients, pure vector clarity
- Lines: Uniform thin black strokes, precise geometric construction, dashed fold/action lines
- Typography: Bold sans-serif, minimal text, heavy reliance on visual icons, universal symbols

## When to Use
- Step-by-step assembly or setup instructions
- Process documentation where clarity is paramount
- Multilingual or language-agnostic content
- When minimalism and precision are the visual goal
