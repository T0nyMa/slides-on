# Corporate Memphis

**Look**: Corporate-friendly flat illustration style with Memphis design elements (geometric shapes, squiggles, bold patterns)
**Palette**: Corporate blues and grays as base, with Memphis accents (coral, teal, mustard, lavender)
**Mood**: Professional yet approachable, contemporary, polished, friendly

## Characteristics
- Texture: Flat with subtle gradients, soft drop shadows, clean surfaces
- Lines: Clean geometric outlines mixed with playful squiggles and dots, consistent stroke weight
- Typography: Clean sans-serif, generous spacing, mix of regular and bold weights

## When to Use
- Enterprise software or SaaS presentations
- Internal corporate communications
- When you need to balance professionalism with visual interest
- Tech company overviews and annual reports
