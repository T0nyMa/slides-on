# Storybook Watercolor

**Look**: Children's book watercolor illustration with soft washes, gentle color bleeds, and dreamy textures
**Palette**: Soft watercolor washes (sky blue, blush pink, sage green, warm yellow), white space, gentle color blending
**Mood**: Dreamy, gentle, imaginative, warm, nostalgic

## Characteristics
- Texture: Watercolor paper grain, pigment blooms and bleeds, wet-on-wet blending, salt-texture effects
- Lines: Loose ink outlines over washes, variable line weight, sketchy under-drawing visible through paint
- Typography: Storybook serif, slightly irregular, hand-lettered titles, gentle curved baselines

## When to Use
- Storytelling and narrative presentations
- Early childhood or education topics
- Creative writing or literary content
- When a soft, emotional approach is more effective than hard data
