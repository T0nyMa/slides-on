# Kawaii

**Look**: Cute Japanese kawaii style with adorable rounded characters, pastels, and cheerful expressions
**Palette**: Soft pastels (baby pink, mint, lavender, butter yellow), warm whites, gentle coral accents
**Mood**: Cheerful, adorable, heartwarming, innocent, uplifting

## Characteristics
- Texture: Smooth and soft, glossy highlights, puffy cloud-like shapes, sparkle effects
- Lines: Rounded, thick outlines with no sharp corners, small decorative elements (stars, hearts, sparkles)
- Typography: Rounded bubble fonts, cute decorative glyphs, emoji-like icons, small and friendly

## When to Use
- Content aimed at young audiences or families
- Lightening a heavy topic with a gentle approach
- Consumer apps with playful branding
- Celebration or appreciation presentations
