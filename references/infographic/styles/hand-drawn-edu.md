# Hand-Drawn Edu

**Look**: Hand-drawn educational illustrations with sketchy lines, notebook doodles, and margin notes
**Palette**: Notebook paper white/cream, ink black/blue, highlighter yellows and pinks, pencil gray
**Mood**: Studious, approachable, personal, informal learning

## Characteristics
- Texture: Paper grain, pencil shading, highlighter streaks, eraser smudges
- Lines: Sketchy, slightly uneven strokes, multiple overlapping lines, quick gesture quality
- Typography: Handwriting, mixed print/cursive, margin annotations, doodle embellishments

## When to Use
- Educational content for non-academic audiences
- Study guides and learning materials
- Explainer content where approachability matters
- "Drawn during the meeting" live-scribing feel
