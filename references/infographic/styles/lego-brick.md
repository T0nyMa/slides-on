# LEGO Brick

**Look**: LEGO brick construction aesthetic with studded blocks, interlocking connections, and vibrant brick colors
**Palette**: Classic LEGO colors (bright red, yellow, blue, green), black outlines, gray baseplates
**Mood**: Constructive, playful, modular, creative, nostalgic

## Characteristics
- Texture: Plastic sheen, stud bumps on top, slight beveled edges, injection-mold marks
- Lines: Crisp geometric block edges, uniform stud grid patterns, interlocking seams
- Typography: Bold blocky lettering, LEGO-style rounded square characters, 3D extruded text bricks

## When to Use
- Modular system or building-block concept explanations
- Team building or construction metaphor presentations
- Product architecture built from components
- When "building something together" is the core message
