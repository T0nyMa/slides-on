# Chalkboard — AI Image Style Definition

Classroom blackboard aesthetic with chalk drawing texture. Dark green-black board background, white and colored chalk marks, chalk dust texture. Conveys teaching authority with approachable warmth.

## Style Lock (跨页复用)

```text
Dark green-black chalkboard (near #1A3A2A) with authentic chalk drawing texture.
All lines drawn in chalk: white chalk #E8E8E8 as primary, colored chalk for emphasis — pale yellow #F5E6A3, sky blue #7EC8E3, pink #F0A8B8, light green #A8D8B9, orange #F5C26B.
Chalk texture throughout: slightly rough powdery edges, variable opacity, subtle smudge marks.
Erased ghost marks (faint chalk residue) near edges for atmosphere. Tiny chalk dust speckles near bottom.
Hand-drawn quality with natural chalk-on-board wobble — never perfectly smooth.
Central diagram compact: 50–60% page width, 35–45% page height, centered with generous dark board margins.
Chalk-drawn rounded boxes, chalk arrows, chalk star marks. Wooden frame edge optionally visible at bottom.
No clean vector graphics, no pure black background, no bright neon, no whiteboard, no photorealistic 3D chalk.
No printed fonts — everything looks chalk-drawn on a real blackboard.
```

## Visual Elements

- Chalk texture on ALL lines — slightly rough, powdery edges, variable opacity
- Chalk smudge marks and erased ghost areas (subtle, atmospheric)
- Chalk dust particles near the bottom edge (tiny white speckles)
- Hand-drawn quality with slight wobble — chalk on board is naturally irregular
- Simple chalk diagrams: boxes with rounded corners, curved arrows, star marks
- Chalk dots and dashed lines for annotation connectors
- Chalk-drawn stick figures (teacher pointing at board) — max 1 per page
- Wooden frame edge visible at bottom (optional, subtle)
- Chalk tray at bottom with spare chalk pieces (optional decorative element)
- Underline emphasis drawn with colored chalk, slightly smeared

## Typography

- ALL text chalk-drawn on blackboard — powdery edges, slightly irregular
- Main titles: bold white chalk, centered, with chalk underline in accent color
- Keywords: written in colored chalk (blue, pink, yellow) for emphasis
- Labels: small white chalk, inside chalk-drawn rounded boxes
- NO printed fonts, NO clean vector text — must have chalk texture
- NO calligraphic brush style — simple chalk lettering, like a teacher writing
- Title: 5–12 Chinese characters
- Labels: 2–5 per page, 2–6 characters each

## Composition

- Dark board occupies the full canvas — immersive classroom feel
- Content area centered with generous board margins (20–25% whitespace = dark board space)
- Title at top, centered, white chalk with colored underline
- Central diagram compact: 50–60% page width and 35–45% page height
- Chalk dust and smudge marks at edges for atmosphere
- Fixed shell: board color, chalk texture, title style, board margins
- Vary only central diagram across pages

## Negative Constraints

- NO clean vector graphics, NO smooth bezier curves — everything must look chalk-drawn
- NO pure black background — use dark green-black `#1A3A2A`
- NO bright neon colors — chalk is muted, slightly dusty
- NO photorealistic 3D chalk — flat chalk drawing style
- NO whiteboard (white background) — this is a BLACKBOARD
- NO many characters, NO crowded composition
- NO printed fonts disguised as chalk — texture and irregularity are essential
- NO fake English, NO gibberish, NO watermarks
- NO gradients (except subtle chalk smudge gradients, which are atmospheric)
