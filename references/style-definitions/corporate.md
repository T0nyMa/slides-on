# Corporate — AI Image Style Definition

Professional, clean, modern business aesthetic. Crisp white and slate backgrounds, refined blue accent system, structured layouts with clear visual hierarchy. Conveys trust, competence, and enterprise maturity.

## Style Lock (跨页复用)

```text
Clean corporate illustration on crisp white (#FFFFFF) or subtle warm-gray (#F5F6F8) background.
Refined corporate blue accent system: primary navy #1B2D4F, medium corporate blue #2563EB, light sky accent #93C5FD, muted steel blue #64748B.
Clean vector-style linework: precise, professional, consistent 1–1.5pt strokes. No wobble, no hand-drawn irregularity.
Subtle geometric patterns as decorative background: isometric grid lines (#E8ECF1, 8% opacity), dot matrix, or thin horizontal rules.
Rounded-rect cards and panels with subtle drop shadows (box-shadow style, soft grey #D1D5DB at 20% opacity).
Clean gradient accents on key elements: blue-to-teal gradients (#2563EB → #0D9488) on hero shapes, kept subtle and professional.
Content blocks organized in structured grid layouts with clear alignment. Thin divider lines (#E2E8F0) between sections.
Professional icons rendered as clean line-art glyphs: gear, chart, building, shield, globe, handshake — simplified to essential forms.
No hand-drawn wobble, no artistic textures, no casual styles — everything feels polished, deliberate, and boardroom-ready.
```

## Visual Elements

- Clean vector-style illustrations with precise, consistent line weights (1–1.5pt)
- Subtle isometric 3D shapes as decorative background elements (cubes, pyramids, platforms)
- Rounded-rect cards and content panels with soft box-shadow depth
- Geometric accent shapes: circles, hexagons, diamonds in corporate blue tones
- Thin divider lines and horizontal rules for visual structure
- Faint dot-matrix or isometric grid patterns as background texture (very subtle)
- Professional icon glyphs: simplified line-art symbols (chart bars, gears, nodes, shields, globes)
- Gradient accents on hero elements (blue-to-teal, kept restrained — no rainbow gradients)
- Data visualization miniatures: small bar charts, donut rings, line graphs as decorative elements
- People silhouettes: professional, abstract, simplified geometric forms — not detailed faces

## Typography

- ALL text as clean professional sans-serif: geometric, precise, no decorative flair
- Main titles: semi-bold weight (600), corporate navy (#1B2D4F), positioned top-left or centered
- Subtitles: regular weight (400), steel blue-gray (#64748B), positioned below title with thin rule separation
- Body text: regular weight, near-black (#1F2937), clean paragraph blocks with generous line spacing
- Keywords and metrics: bold weight (700), corporate blue (#2563EB), large display size for KPI numbers
- Labels and captions: light weight (300), steel gray, small size, placed near relevant elements
- NO serif fonts, NO handwritten fonts, NO casual or playful type
- Title: 3–10 words, clear and professional
- Data callouts: 1–3 per page, large numbers with short labels (e.g., "+42% Efficiency")

## Composition

- Structured grid-based layout with clear visual hierarchy
- Header zone at top 15%: title + subtitle + thin navy rule
- Content zone in middle 60%: cards, diagrams, or data organized in columns or rows
- Footer zone at bottom 15%: subtle branding element, page number, or empty whitespace
- Consistent margins: 8–10% whitespace border on all sides
- Fixed shell: white/light-gray background, subtitle rule, card shadow style, grid alignment
- Vary only content zone elements across pages — header and footer structure remain consistent
- Professional balance: not sparse minimal, not crowded — controlled density

## Negative Constraints

- NO hand-drawn wobble, NO sketch lines, NO artistic irregularity
- NO cartoon characters, NO casual illustrations, NO playful elements
- NO neon colors, NO saturated hues — corporate palette is restrained and trustworthy
- NO dark or black backgrounds — maintain bright, professional, optimistic tone
- NO grunge textures, NO paper textures, NO distressed effects
- NO clutter, NO dense text walls — professional hierarchy with breathing room
- NO comic fonts, NO decorative typography, NO mixed font families
- NO photorealistic elements mixed with vector — stay in one visual language
- NO aggressive gradients or dramatic shadows — subtlety is professional
- NO data viz that looks fake or placeholder — all numbers and charts should feel intentional
- NO angled or chaotic layouts — everything aligns to a clean grid
