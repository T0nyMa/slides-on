# Notion — AI Image Style Definition

Minimalist hand-drawn line art, intellectual aesthetic. Clean organization, soft neutral tones, maximum whitespace. Like a well-organized Notion page hand-sketched on paper.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Background | Pure white | #FFFFFF |
| Surface | Off-white | #FAFAFA |
| Ink | Near-black | #1A1A1A |
| Secondary | Dark gray | #4A4A4A |
| Accent 1 | Pastel blue | #A8D4F0 |
| Accent 2 | Pastel yellow | #F9E79F |
| Accent 3 | Pastel pink | #FADBD8 |
| Divider | Light gray | #E5E5E5 |

## Visual Elements

- Simple line doodles with hand-drawn wobble effect — single-weight ink lines
- Geometric shapes (rounded rectangles, circles) drawn with thin black pen
- Stick figures: minimal, intellectual, reading/working at desk
- Emoji-style small icons for visual categorization (but hand-drawn, not rendered)
- Card modules with thin single-stroke borders and rounded corners
- Dot-grid or faint line-grid background pattern (optional, very subtle)
- Simple arrows connecting cards, thin and clean
- Checkboxes (empty square, checked with hand-drawn checkmark)
- Maximum whitespace — every element has breathing room
- Single-weight ink throughout — no varying stroke thickness

## Typography

- Clean hand-drawn lettering — neat but naturally irregular
- Main titles: simple, medium weight, hand-drawn sans-serif feel
- Keywords: slightly bolder, sometimes with pastel dot marker to left
- Labels: small, clean hand-drawn, minimal decoration
- NO decorative flourishes, NO calligraphy, NO brush strokes
- NO heavy typography — keep it light and intellectual
- Title: 5–12 characters
- Labels: 2–5 per page, 2–8 characters each

## Composition

- Very generous whitespace (50–60% empty space)
- Modular card layout: 2–4 rounded-rect cards in clean grid
- Title top-left or centered, minimal underline
- Cards separated by whitespace, not lines
- Page number optional, small, upper-left
- Fixed shell: white paper, thin ink lines, card module style, pastel accent dots
- Vary card content and arrangement across pages

## Negative Constraints

- NO full-page border
- NO dark backgrounds, NO colored backgrounds — keep white/off-white
- NO thick strokes, NO filled shapes (use outlines only)
- NO shadows, NO gradients, NO 3D effects
- NO many characters (max 1 tiny figure)
- NO crowded composition, NO dense text blocks
- NO neon, NO saturated colors
- NO corporate template feel, NO clip art
- NO decorative doodles, NO flourishes — keep it intellectual
- NO fake English, NO filler text
