# Sketch Notes — AI Image Style Definition

Hand-drawn educational infographic with wobble lines, pastel marker fills, paper texture. Warm, approachable, like a well-crafted teaching notebook.

## Visual Elements

- Hand-drawn wobble on ALL lines and shapes — stable but slightly irregular, never perfectly straight
- Thin ink pen lines (0.3–0.5mm visual weight), black-brown `#3D2B1F`
- Simple stick-figure characters at desks, working, thinking (max 1 per page, far corner)
- Curved hand-drawn arrows connecting concepts, slim and quiet
- Rounded-rect frames for content blocks, single-weight outline
- Star-burst marks for key terms (4–6 pointed star, small, coral red)
- Circle-mark and pale underline for emphasis on keywords
- Sparse corner construction marks: faint grey dots, ruler ticks, measurement lines
- Paper texture background — subtle grain, warm cream tone
- Pencil hatching on diagrams for depth (light cross-hatching)
- Props (books, screens, documents) blank or with simple line marks only

## Typography

- ALL text hand-drawn / handwritten style — natural hand-lettering feel
- Main titles: medium weight, prominent, hand-drawn Chinese, with one pale blue hand-drawn underline
- Keywords: bold + enlarged + pastel marker highlighter block behind text
- Labels and annotations: small, readable, hand-drawn, inside small pastel marker labels
- NO realistic or computer-generated fonts
- NO calligraphic brush style, NO dramatic flyaway strokes
- NO childish doodle fonts, NO heavy advertising type
- Title: 5–12 Chinese characters preferred
- Subtitle: 3–12 Chinese characters or three terms separated by ·
- Main labels: 2–5 per page, 2–6 characters each

## Composition

- Large negative space (40–50% whitespace) — calm, premium feel
- Central diagram occupies 50–60% page width and 35–45% page height
- Title centered at top with pale blue hand-drawn underline
- Subtitle centered below title when needed, smaller
- Page number small, upper-left, handwritten style (e.g., `03 / 10`)
- Fixed outer shell across pages: paper tone, title position, underline style, corner marks
- Vary only the central diagram area according to content

## Negative Constraints

- NO full-page border or rectangular frame
- NO yellow paper, beige paper, old paper, or tea-stained paper
- NO photorealistic rendering, NO 3D render, NO computer-generated look
- NO gradients, NO shadows, NO neon colors
- NO thick marker strokes
- NO many characters (max 1 tiny figure, far corner)
- NO bullet dumps, NO dense text blocks, NO long paragraphs
- NO giant title, NO poster-like full-page illustration
- NO heavy bottom quote boxes, NO oversized conclusion boxes
- NO fake English, NO gibberish, NO filler text, NO watermarks
- NO saturated colors, NO product-card styling

## Role-Specific Prompt Fragments

### illustration

```
Visual role: illustration / background image for a slide deck.
The image serves as a visual atmosphere element — NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO words in the image.
Text will be overlaid by the slide rendering engine, so leave clean empty zones.
Main subject positioned slightly off-center, leave 35–45% of the left or top area as text-safe empty space.
Suitable for presentation slide background — not too busy, not too sparse.
Same sketch-notes hand-drawn style: warm cream paper, fine ink lines, pastel marker accents, paper texture.
```

### content-page

```
Visual role: complete content page — this is a standalone deliverable image.
ALL visible Chinese text MUST be hand-drawn style, baked into the final image.
Main titles hand-drawn Chinese, prominent, with pale blue underline.
Keywords emphasized with pastel highlighter blocks behind text.
Labels in small pastel marker boxes.
Keep text short and exact — see Required text only below.
NO printed fonts, NO computer typesetting, NO realistic text rendering.
Text should feel like a person carefully wrote it on paper with pen and marker.
```
