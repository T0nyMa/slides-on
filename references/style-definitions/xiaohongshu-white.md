# Xiaohongshu White — AI Image Style Definition

Clean pastel aesthetic optimized for Xiaohongshu (XHS) and social media content. Soft lighting, lifestyle photography feel, gentle color palettes, and warm approachable compositions. Evokes the curated, aesthetic-driven visual language of Chinese lifestyle social platforms.

## Visual Elements

- Soft diffused lighting throughout — as if illuminated by a large north-facing window on a cloudy day
- Lifestyle flat-lay compositions: curated objects arranged on a surface, viewed from above or at a gentle angle
- Gentle botanical accents: soft-focus flowers, eucalyptus sprigs, dried baby's breath, single monstera leaves
- Delicate decorative marks: tiny hand-drawn stars, hearts, dots, and subtle sparkles scattered near focal areas
- Soft-focus bokeh circles in warm tones floating in the background
- Clean content cards: soft rounded rectangles (12–16pt radius) with nearly transparent warm fills
- Subtle film-grain or paper-fiber texture overlay at very low opacity for tactile warmth
- Gentle warm-tone vignette at image edges — barely perceptible but adds coziness
- Curated object arrangements: ceramic mugs, linen fabric, dried flowers, wooden beads, glass vials — objects that feel personal and aesthetic
- Thin delicate linework: soft taupe (#C4B5A0) or warm gray (#9B9590) lines at 0.5–1pt for borders and dividers
- Subtle gradient washes in the background: barely-there transitions between warm cream and blush tones

## Typography

- ALL text as clean, warm, approachable typography — slightly rounded sans-serif preferred
- Main titles: medium weight (500–600), soft charcoal (#4A4540), displayed prominently with generous letter-spacing
- Keywords and emphasis: rendered in soft coral (#F0B8B0) or muted sage (#B8C9B0) for gentle visual hierarchy
- Subtitles and descriptions: lighter weight, warm gray (#9B9590), positioned with ample whitespace around them
- Labels on objects: very small, delicate, warm gray, treated as decorative elements rather than primary information
- Text often placed on soft cream content cards with subtle rounded corners
- NO bold heavy typography — everything is gentle, warm, and inviting
- NO harsh black text — use soft charcoal (#4A4540) and warm gray (#9B9590)
- NO cold or clinical sans-serif — fonts should feel warm and approachable
- Title: 2–8 words, friendly and aesthetic
- Body: short 1–3 sentence descriptions, never dense paragraphs
- Labels: 2–6 per page, 1–3 words each, decorative and light

## Composition

- Generous whitespace: 45–55% of canvas is untouched warm near-white
- Central focal area: curated flat-lay arrangement or single hero object, occupying 30–40% of canvas
- Asymmetric balance with gentle visual weight distribution
- Content cards positioned with clear breathing room between them
- Decorative elements (stars, sparkles, botanical sprigs) placed at the periphery — framing rather than filling
- Fixed shell: warm background tone, lighting quality, card style, sparkle language, color palette
- Vary the central subject, accent color emphasis, and botanical element choice across pages
- Always leave space for overlay text — this is a content-ready aesthetic

## Negative Constraints

- NO dark backgrounds — always warm near-white, cream, or blush tones
- NO harsh shadows, NO dramatic lighting — lighting must be soft and diffused
- NO saturated or neon colors — palette is pastel, muted, and warm
- NO photorealistic 3D renders — maintain the illustrated/curated aesthetic
- NO cluttered compositions — whitespace is essential to the lifestyle aesthetic
- NO cold or clinical design — everything should feel warm, personal, and inviting
- NO aggressive typography — no heavy black weights, no oversized impact text
- NO busy patterns or dense textures — subtlety and softness are key
- NO corporate or institutional visual language — this is personal and lifestyle-oriented
- NO dark grunge or distressed effects — everything is clean, soft, and cared-for
- NO sharp geometric precision — gentle curves and organic softness throughout
- NO mixed lighting temperatures — consistent warm soft light across the entire image
